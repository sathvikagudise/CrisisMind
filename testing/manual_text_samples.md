# Manual Testing — Text Samples

Use these descriptions for **Text Only** and **Multimodal** analysis testing.

## Little Damage (5 samples)

1. "The road has a few visible cracks but traffic is normal."
2. "Some tree branches fell on the lawn but the house is intact."
3. "Light rain caused minor puddles on the street."
4. "A few roof tiles shifted slightly during the storm."
5. "The fence has some minor scratches from wind-blown debris."

## Mild Damage (5 samples)

6. "Several buildings have broken windows and partial wall damage."
7. "Floodwater has entered the ground floor of the apartment complex."
8. "The church roof collapsed partially due to heavy snow load."
9. "Moderate cracks in the retaining wall along the highway."
10. "Three vehicles damaged by falling tree branches after the storm."

## Severe Damage (5 samples)

11. "Multiple buildings have collapsed and rescue teams are evacuating people."
12. "Entire neighbourhood destroyed by the wildfire leaving only ashes."
13. "The hospital has severe structural damage and is being evacuated."
14. "Hundreds of homes washed away by the catastrophic flood waters."
15. "Major earthquake has reduced the city center to complete rubble."
